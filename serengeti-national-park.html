Planning Your Visit

The right time
for your safari.

The Serengeti changes throughout the year. The best time to visit depends on what you hope to experience, which areas you want to explore, and how you prefer to travel.

Ask Us About Your Dates

Jan – Mar

Calving Season in the Southern Plains

Parts of the southern Serengeti and Ndutu area can offer opportunities to observe wildebeest calving, with predator activity often drawing wildlife enthusiasts. Conditions and herd locations vary from year to year.

Apr – May

Green Landscapes & Fewer Crowds

The longer rains can bring lush scenery and atmospheric photography conditions. Some roads may be more challenging, and travel plans should account for seasonal weather.

Jun – Jul

Dry Season & Herd Movements

As conditions become drier, wildlife viewing can be rewarding in suitable areas. Herd movements vary, and the best location depends on the migration's progress.

Aug – Oct

Northern Serengeti & River Crossings

The northern Serengeti can be an important migration area during this period. River crossings may occur, but their timing and location are unpredictable and cannot be guaranteed.

Nov – Dec

Short Rains & Changing Landscapes

Seasonal rains begin to transform parts of the ecosystem, while wildlife movements continue. This period can suit travellers looking for a different perspective on the Serengeti.

Seasonal patterns are approximate. Weather and wildlife movements vary. We recommend confirming current conditions and planning around your specific travel dates.

Before You Travel

Good to know.

A few helpful answers as you begin planning your Serengeti safari.

How many days should I spend in the Serengeti? +

Three to four nights can provide a useful introduction, while longer stays may allow more time to explore different areas or focus on seasonal wildlife movements. Your ideal duration depends on your interests, travel route, and budget.

Can I see the Great Migration? +

The migration moves through the greater Serengeti ecosystem during the year. The best area to visit depends on your travel dates and recent herd movements. We recommend planning around the relevant season, while understanding that exact sightings and river crossings cannot be guaranteed.

Is the Serengeti suitable for a luxury safari? +

Yes. Accommodation options range from comfortable tented camps to high-end lodges and exclusive wilderness retreats. Availability, location, amenities, and pricing depend on your dates and selected properties.

Can I combine the Serengeti with other destinations? +

Yes. Depending on your schedule, you may be able to combine the Serengeti with destinations such as Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire National Park, Lake Manyara, or a Zanzibar beach stay. The best combination depends on travel time and your preferred pace.

How much does a Serengeti safari cost? +

Costs vary according to the season, accommodation, length of stay, group size, transport, and included activities. Share your approximate budget with us so suitable options can be explored before you commit to a booking.

What should I bring on safari? +

Lightweight neutral-coloured clothing, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, insect repellent, binoculars, and a camera are useful starting points. Your final packing list should reflect the season, itinerary, and accommodation arrangements.
