1. THE SERENGETI, PRIVATELY Page purpose This should be the strongest safari-focused page. It should make someone imagine what a private Serengeti journey feels like rather than simply listing hotels and activities. Recommended page structure Hero THE SERENGETI, PRIVATELY A private safari through Tanzania's most iconic wilderness. Supporting copy: There is a difference between seeing the Serengeti and experiencing it on your own terms. Kavora designs private safari journeys around your pace, your interests and the way you want to encounter the wilderness. CTA: Begin Designing Your Journey 01 — The Idea A safari designed around you. The Serengeti does not need a rigid itinerary. Your days can begin slowly with coffee overlooking the plains, move into the wilderness when the light is right, and end beneath an open African sky. Whether your priority is wildlife, photography, landscape, conservation, solitude or simply time away from everything, the journey is shaped accordingly. Kavora handles the details behind the experience so that the journey itself feels effortless. 02 — The Experience The Serengeti, beyond the expected. Potential experience blocks: Private game drives Sunrise and sunset safaris Great Migration experiences Wildlife photography Remote wilderness locations Private bush dining Cultural encounters Walking experiences where appropriate Hot-air balloon experience Exclusive-use or intimate safari camps Conservation-focused experiences The page should explain these rather than just listing them. For example: Private Game Drives Leave on your own schedule, spend longer with the sightings that matter to you, and let the day develop naturally rather than following a predetermined route. 03 — Where You Might Go Possible journey flow: Arusha → Serengeti → Ngorongoro → Tarangire / additional wilderness But present this as: One possible rhythm rather than: Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3 That preserves the luxury/custom nature. 04 — The Places You Might Stay Introduce accommodation according to character: The Classic Safari Camp For an intimate, traditional safari atmosphere. The Private Wilderness Retreat For greater privacy and a more secluded experience. The Contemporary Safari Lodge For travellers looking for sophisticated design without losing connection to the landscape. Then say: Your accommodation is selected according to the journey we create together—not simply from a predetermined hotel list. 05 — A Day in the Serengeti This is an important editorial section. Show a cinematic example: 05:30 — First Light Coffee. Stillness. The first movement across the plains. 06:00 — Into the Wilderness A private vehicle leaves camp while the landscape wakes. 10:30 — Slow Morning Return to camp or continue exploring depending on the day's rhythm. 13:00 — Lunch A considered meal overlooking the wilderness. 16:00 — Afternoon Safari Return to the plains as the light begins to soften. 19:00 — Under the African Sky Dinner, conversation and the sounds of the wilderness. End with: And tomorrow does not have to look like today. 06 — When to Go Explain seasons: Dry season Green season Great Migration Calving season Predator activity Shoulder seasons But avoid promising a particular wildlife sighting. 07 — Designed Around You A simple preference section: For the photographer More time in the field and flexibility around light and wildlife. For the couple Privacy, intimacy and carefully chosen stays. For the family A comfortable pace and experiences suitable for different generations. For the returning traveller Less obvious routes and deeper experiences beyond the classic safari circuit. 08 — Begin the Conversation Your Serengeti does not have to look like anyone else's. CTA: Start Designing Your Journey 2. FROM SAVANNAH TO THE INDIAN OCEAN This should be your Safari + Zanzibar combination page. Hero FROM SAVANNAH TO THE INDIAN OCEAN A private journey from the wilderness of Tanzania to the shores of Zanzibar. Supporting copy: Begin among the great landscapes of northern Tanzania before exchanging the rhythm of the savannah for warm Indian Ocean waters. One journey. Two completely different worlds. 01 — The Concept From wilderness to water. Start with Tanzania's wildlife-rich landscapes. Then slow everything down. After days of safari, the journey moves towards Zanzibar—white sand, warm water, historic streets and long evenings by the ocean. The contrast is what makes the journey. 02 — The Safari Potential destinations: Serengeti Wildlife and expansive landscapes. Ngorongoro The dramatic crater and one of Tanzania's most distinctive ecosystems. Tarangire Ancient baobabs, elephants and a quieter safari atmosphere. Again, these should be presented as possibilities, not mandatory stops. 03 — The Transition This could be a beautiful editorial section. From dust to salt air. One moment you are watching elephants move across the plains. The next, you are walking barefoot beside the Indian Ocean. The transition itself becomes part of the journey. 04 — Zanzibar Break Zanzibar into experiences. The Coast Private beaches, swimming, sailing and slow afternoons. Stone Town History, architecture, markets and culture. The Indian Ocean Snorkelling, diving, dhow sailing and marine experiences. Island Dining Private dinners, beachfront meals and locally influenced cuisine. 05 — How the Journey Might Flow Example: Arusha Arrival and preparation. ↓ Serengeti Private safari. ↓ Ngorongoro Landscape, wildlife and culture. ↓ Zanzibar Ocean, relaxation and exploration. ↓ Departure Return home refreshed rather than rushed. Again, label this: One possible journey 06 — The Balance This section should sell the emotional value. Adventure without exhaustion. The itinerary is designed so that moments of intensity are balanced with time to slow down. Safari mornings. Long lunches. Ocean afternoons. Sunset drinks. Unhurried dinners. The goal is not to see everything. It is to experience the right things well. 07 — Who This Journey Suits Couples Honeymooners Families Multi-generational travellers First-time visitors to Tanzania Travellers wanting both safari and beach 08 — CTA Two landscapes.
