01 / The destination

A world above
the ordinary.

In north-western Rwanda, the slopes of the Virunga Mountains rise through cloud and rainforest. Bamboo thickets, volcanic peaks and rich montane habitats create a setting unlike any other on the continent.

The defining encounter is a trek into the forest to spend a carefully managed hour observing mountain gorillas in their natural habitat. Yet the experience need not end there. Golden monkey tracking, mountain hikes, conservation history and secluded lodge stays can all become part of a journey shaped around you.

Kavora brings these elements together with considered pacing, thoughtful accommodation choices and the practical details handled before you arrive.

03 / Finding your moment

The right season.
Your kind of journey.

Gorilla trekking is possible throughout the year. Seasonal rainfall, trail conditions and your preferred experiences all help determine when to travel.

June — September

The drier months

Often preferred for trekking because trails may be less muddy. This is a popular period, so advance planning is important.

December — February

A second drier window

Another popular period for forest trekking and combining Volcanoes National Park with other parts of Rwanda.

March — May

The greener season

Rain can make trails more challenging. The landscape is lush, but weather and walking conditions need careful consideration.

October — November

A quieter rhythm

A wetter period that can suit flexible travellers who are comfortable with changing mountain weather.

Seasonal guidance is indicative. Conditions vary, and no season guarantees a particular wildlife sighting or trail condition.

07 / Before you go

A few things
to know.

The right preparation makes a meaningful difference in a mountain environment. These are the essentials we consider when planning your stay.

Ask a concierge ↗

Gorilla trekking requires a valid permit for your chosen date. We can help coordinate the permit process and align it with your accommodation and transport plans. Availability should be confirmed before travel arrangements are finalised.

The right length depends on your activities and how much time you want to spend at your lodge. A short stay can focus on gorilla trekking; a longer visit allows additional experiences and a more relaxed pace. We plan around your actual dates rather than a standard package duration.

Trek duration and terrain vary according to the gorilla family and conditions. Expect uneven, sometimes steep or muddy forest trails. Tell us about your fitness, mobility and preferences so we can help you prepare and confirm what is suitable with the relevant operator.

Sturdy walking shoes, long trousers, layers, a lightweight waterproof jacket and protection for your hands and lower legs are useful. The mountain climate can be cool and wet. We can provide a trip-specific packing list before departure.
