Section 03

Personal information we may collect

The information collected depends on the service requested and may include:

Contact details

Name, email address, telephone number, country of residence and preferred communication method.

Travel and booking details

Proposed dates, destinations, itinerary, accommodation preferences, flight details, booking references and information about other travellers.

Identification information

Passport details, nationality, date of birth or other travel-document information where necessary for a booking or legal requirement.

Preferences and special requests

Dietary requirements, room preferences, interests, celebrations, accessibility arrangements and other details voluntarily provided for the journey.

Payment and transaction information

Invoice details, payment status, transaction references and billing information. Payment-card details should be handled through appropriate payment providers and not collected by Kavora unnecessarily.

Communications

Information provided through email, telephone, messaging services, enquiry forms or other contact channels.

Technical and website information

Device and browser details, usage information, IP address, cookie identifiers and similar data where the website actually collects it.

We aim to collect information that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose. Please do not send information that is not needed for your enquiry or booking.

Section 04

How we collect information

We may collect information:

  • Directly from you when you contact us, submit an enquiry or request a quotation.
  • When you confirm a booking or request concierge assistance.
  • From a person who is authorised to arrange travel on your behalf.
  • From suppliers or service providers where necessary to manage or verify a booking.
  • Automatically through the website, but only to the extent enabled by its actual technologies and settings.
  • From other lawful sources where necessary for the requested service, fraud prevention or a legal obligation.

If you provide information about another traveller, you should be authorised to do so and should provide that person with any notice required by applicable law.

Section 05

Why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and prepare tailored quotations.
  • Design, reserve, coordinate and manage requested travel arrangements.
  • Communicate itinerary details, booking updates, travel information and service notices.
  • Accommodate relevant preferences and special requests.
  • Manage invoices, payments and refunds where applicable.
  • Handle complaints, service issues and emergency arrangements.
  • Maintain business records, protect the security of services, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • Send marketing communications where lawful and where any required consent has been obtained.

We will not use personal information for a materially unrelated purpose without an appropriate legal basis and any notice or permission required by law.

Section 06

Legal basis for processing

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, the basis may include taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing a legitimate interest where permitted, or obtaining consent where required. The relevant basis depends on the purpose and circumstances of the processing.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent as provided by applicable law. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully undertaken before withdrawal. If you do not provide information necessary for a requested booking, we may be unable to arrange the relevant service.

Section 07

Sensitive information and children

Health-related details, accessibility needs and information concerning children may receive additional legal protection. We ask that such information be provided only where relevant to the requested arrangements. Where required, we will obtain appropriate consent or establish another lawful basis and apply suitable safeguards. Access should be limited to people who need the information for the relevant service or lawful purpose.

Where information about a child is necessary for a booking, travel document, safety requirement or other legitimate service purpose, it should be provided or authorised by a parent, guardian or another person legally entitled to act on the child’s behalf, as required by applicable law. We will not knowingly use children’s information for unrelated promotional purposes without the required lawful basis and permissions.

Section 08

When we share information

To provide the requested service, we may share relevant information with:

  • Airlines, flight-booking providers and other transport providers.
  • Hotels, lodges, camps and other accommodation providers.
  • Safari operators, guides, activity providers and ground transport companies.
  • Airport assistance, dining, concierge and other service providers.
  • Payment processors, booking systems, website hosting, communications and technology providers.
  • Professional advisers, insurers or auditors where relevant and lawful.
  • Public authorities or other recipients where disclosure is required or otherwise permitted by law.

We aim to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and to use appropriate contractual or other safeguards where required. A supplier receiving information may process it under its own privacy policy and legal obligations. Where you contract directly with a provider, review that provider’s terms and privacy notice.

Kavora does not sell clients’ personal information.

Section 09

International transfers

International travel may require information to be shared with suppliers or systems located in other countries. Before such transfers occur, Kavora must assess and follow the requirements of applicable law, which may include an authorisation or permit, safeguards, notices or other steps. A transfer will not be treated as automatically permitted merely because it relates to travel.

Before publication, Kavora should identify the countries and providers to which personal data may be transferred and verify the applicable Tanzanian requirements and any other laws that apply to its activities.

Section 10

Security and confidentiality

Kavora aims to use reasonable technical, organisational and administrative measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the risks involved. Measures may include restricted access, secure account management, suitable password practices, secure transmission methods, confidentiality obligations and careful selection of service providers.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a personal-data incident occurs, Kavora will respond and provide any notification required by applicable law. A public Policy should be supported by practical internal procedures; wording alone does not secure information.

Section 11

How long we retain information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, regulatory, accounting, contractual and dispute-related obligations. The appropriate period may differ by record type and booking.

Before launch, Kavora should establish an internal retention schedule for enquiry records, booking files, invoices, identity documents, marketing records and website logs. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise dispose of it securely, as permitted or required by law.

Section 12

Cookies, analytics and similar technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand performance or measure marketing activity. The technologies actually used depend on the website’s configuration.

Before publication, Kavora must audit the live website for cookies, analytics tools, embedded maps or videos, social media integrations, advertising pixels and other third-party scripts. Where consent is required for non-essential technologies, an appropriate consent mechanism must be implemented. Browser settings may also allow you to manage cookies, although disabling some cookies may affect functionality.
