A different side of the island

Where the island
slows down.

Beyond Zanzibar's historic streets and northern beaches lies a quieter landscape shaped by tropical forest, traditional villages and a coastline that feels wonderfully unhurried.

The south invites you to experience the island at a gentler pace. Walk beneath the canopy of Jozani Forest, encounter the wildlife that makes this ecosystem unique, discover coastal traditions, and make time for the simple pleasure of being here.

At Kavora, we bring these moments together into considered journeys, balancing nature, local culture and restorative time by the ocean.

Jozani Forest.

Step into one of Zanzibar's most remarkable natural environments. Jozani is celebrated for its rich tropical woodland, raw biodiversity, shifting mangrove ecosystems, and the rare endemic Zanzibar red colobus monkey.

Known for

Colobus Monkeys · Boardwalks · Guided Walks

Kizimkazi by the sea.

On Zanzibar's southern coast, Kizimkazi offers a glimpse of a long-standing relationship between island communities and the sea. Traditional fishing culture, raw coastal scenery, and pristine open horizons give this village a character completely its own.

Known for

Coastal Villages · Ocean Excursions · Seascapes

Makunduchi and beyond.

Discover a more everyday expression of Zanzibar in the island's southern villages. Here, the pace of life is shaped by community, the seasons, fishing, farming, and the quiet traditions carried intentionally between generations.

Known for

Swahili Heritage · Village Life · Crafts

A few useful details

Before you set off.

A little preparation helps make your time in southern Zanzibar more comfortable, considered and rewarding.

Zanzibar can be visited throughout much of the year. The drier periods, generally June to October and January to February, are popular for outdoor activities. Rainfall, humidity and sea conditions vary, so we recommend planning around your preferred experiences and checking conditions closer to departure.

Many visitors allow around two to three hours for a guided forest visit, including time for the main trails and mangrove area when accessible. Your final schedule will depend on your guide, route, weather and onward plans.

Jozani is one of the best-known places to learn about and observe the endemic Zanzibar red colobus monkey. They are wild animals, however, so sightings and viewing conditions cannot be guaranteed. Visitors should follow guide instructions and avoid feeding or disturbing wildlife.
