The Northern Shore

A different rhythm
of island living.

At the northern tip of Zanzibar, Nungwi and Kendwa offer a different expression of island life. The Indian Ocean shifts through shades of blue, traditional dhow sails cross the horizon, and the day unfolds at its own pace.

Nungwi brings together coastal culture, fishing traditions, ocean excursions and a lively village atmosphere. Nearby Kendwa offers a more open stretch of sand, spacious beach settings and sunsets that invite you to stay a little longer.

With Kavora, these two neighbouring destinations become one considered journey, shaped around your interests, preferred pace and idea of the perfect escape.

Two Shores, One Island

The character
of Nungwi.

Nungwi is a coastal community where the island's relationship with the sea remains part of everyday life. Wooden dhows, traditional boatbuilding, fishing culture and warm ocean evenings give this northern village a character beyond its beaches.

Explore the shoreline, arrange a private dhow excursion, discover local traditions or simply allow the hours to unfold between the water and the village.

Before You Arrive

A few things
worth knowing.

A little preparation leaves more room to enjoy the island. Here are some useful things to consider when planning your stay in Nungwi and Kendwa.

Zanzibar is a year-round destination. June to October generally brings drier weather, while January and February are also popular for warm, sunny conditions. March to May is typically wetter. The right time depends on your priorities and preferred activities.

Nungwi has a stronger village atmosphere, with fishing traditions, boatbuilding and a wider mix of coastal activities. Kendwa is known for its broad sandy beach and relaxed beach-resort setting. Both can be combined in one northern Zanzibar itinerary.
