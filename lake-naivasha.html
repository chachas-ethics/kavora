01 / The Destination

A different kind of wild.

Set within Kenya’s Great Rift Valley, Lake Naivasha offers a striking contrast to the open plains of a traditional safari. Its freshwater setting draws an abundance of birds, supports resident hippos and provides a natural setting for slower, more intimate encounters with the landscape.

Days here might begin on the water, continue along quiet lakeside paths and end in a secluded retreat beneath the trees. It is a destination that rewards curiosity rather than a hurried itinerary.

03 / Timing Your Visit

The right time to arrive.

Lake Naivasha can be visited throughout the year. Your preferred experiences, road conditions and the seasonal weather can help determine the best time for your stay.

January — March

Generally drier

Often a good period for outdoor activities, boat trips and exploring nearby landscapes. Conditions vary, so daily planning still matters.

April — May

Long rains

Greener scenery can make this period appealing. Rain may affect roads and outdoor plans, so flexible scheduling and suitable transport are important.

June — October

Often drier

A popular window for combining lakeside experiences with walking, cycling and wider Rift Valley exploration.

November — December

Short rains

A changing landscape and quieter moments may appeal to travellers who prefer a flexible pace. Rainfall varies from year to year.
