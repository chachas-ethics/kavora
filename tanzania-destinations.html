Serengeti National Park

One of Africa's most celebrated wildlife landscapes, the Serengeti is known for its vast grasslands, exceptional predator sightings, and the spectacular seasonal movement of wildebeest and zebra. Every game drive offers a new perspective on life in the wild.

Known for: Great Migration, lions, cheetahs, expansive savannah.

Explore The Serengeti

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Formed by a vast volcanic caldera, Ngorongoro brings dramatic scenery and remarkable wildlife together in one extraordinary setting. Its crater floor supports a rich variety of animals, while the surrounding highlands provide sweeping views and opportunities to experience the region's cultural heritage.

Known for: Crater landscapes, wildlife viewing, black rhinos, highland scenery.

Explore Ngorongoro

Lake Manyara National Park

Set beneath the Great Rift Valley escarpment, Lake Manyara offers a changing landscape of groundwater forest, open plains, and lakeshore. Its diverse habitats attract elephants, abundant birdlife, and the park's famously elusive tree-climbing lions.

Known for: Birdlife, elephants, forest scenery, tree-climbing lions.

Explore Lake Manyara

Tarangire National Park

Tarangire is a landscape of sculptural baobabs, golden grasslands, and the Tarangire River, which becomes especially important to wildlife during the dry season. Its elephant herds and quieter atmosphere make it a distinctive addition to a northern Tanzania safari.

Known for: Elephant herds, baobabs, seasonal wildlife concentrations.

Explore Tarangire

Arusha National Park

Smaller in scale but rich in variety, Arusha National Park combines forest, crater lakes, open grasslands, and views of Mount Meru. Walking safaris and canoeing opportunities offer a more intimate way to experience Tanzania's landscapes beyond the traditional vehicle-based safari.

Known for: Mount Meru scenery, giraffes, colobus monkeys, walking safaris.

Explore Arusha National Park
