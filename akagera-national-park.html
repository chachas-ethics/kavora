01 / The destination

Rwanda’s wild
side revealed.

In eastern Rwanda, Akagera unfolds across grasslands, rolling woodland, wetlands and a chain of lakes fed by the Akagera River. It is the country’s defining savannah safari destination and a remarkable contrast to its mist-covered mountain forests.

The park is home to the Big Five—lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino—alongside giraffes, zebras, antelope, hippos, crocodiles and a rich diversity of birdlife. Sightings are never guaranteed, but every drive offers a different view of this varied landscape.

With Kavora, the experience extends beyond the game drive. We help bring together the right guides, carefully selected accommodation, lake excursions and private transfers into a seamless journey.

03 / Finding your moment

Follow the
seasons.

Akagera can be explored throughout the year. Seasonal conditions influence road access, landscapes and wildlife viewing, so the best time depends on the experiences you value most.

June — September

The drier season

Often favoured for game viewing as vegetation can be less dense and animals may gather around available water sources.

December — February

A second drier window

A useful period to consider for classic safari activities, with weather and conditions checked against your exact dates.

March — May

The greener months

Greener scenery and wetter conditions can bring a different character to the landscape. Road conditions may require additional planning.

October — November

A changing landscape

Seasonal rains may refresh the scenery and appeal to birders. We plan around current weather, access and your preferred activities.

These are general seasonal patterns, not guarantees of animal sightings, road conditions or weather. Conditions vary from year to year.
