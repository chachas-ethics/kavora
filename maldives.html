When To Visit

An island escape, in every season.

The Maldives is a year-round destination, with changing seasonal conditions shaping the character of each visit. Your ideal travel period depends on the experiences, atmosphere and pace you have in mind.

November – April

Drier Season

Generally calmer conditions, sunnier days and excellent opportunities for beach time, sailing and outdoor experiences.

May – October

Green Season

A season of changing skies, occasional rainfall and rich marine encounters. Conditions vary, offering a different perspective on island life.

Year-Round

Warm tropical temperatures make the Maldives an inviting escape throughout the year. We help align your dates with the experience you want.

Before You Go

A few things to know.

A little preparation makes for a more seamless island experience.

International visitors typically arrive through Velana International Airport near Malé. Depending on your chosen island, onward transfers may be arranged by speedboat, domestic flight or seaplane.

Transfers between islands are generally arranged through the resort or accommodation provider. The transfer method and journey time depend on the location of your stay.

The Maldives has a warm tropical climate throughout the year. Lightweight clothing, swimwear, sun protection and comfortable footwear are useful for most island stays.

The Maldives is a Muslim country. Visitors should dress respectfully when visiting inhabited local islands. Resort islands generally have their own guest guidelines.

The ideal length of stay depends on your chosen island, travel style and planned experiences. We can help coordinate your accommodation, transfers and activities around your preferred dates.
