When to Go

The right time is the time that suits you.

The Maldives is a year-round destination. Conditions vary through the seasons, so your preferred experiences and island location should guide the timing.

Seasonal descriptions are general guidance, not a weather guarantee. Conditions can vary between atolls and from year to year. We recommend confirming local conditions for your travel dates.
