When to Go

An island for every kind of escape.

Mauritius has a generally warm tropical climate, with warmer, wetter months and cooler, drier months. The right season depends on your activities and preferred conditions.

Seasonal descriptions are general guidance. Local weather can vary, and conditions may differ between coastal areas and the interior. Confirm forecasts and activity conditions close to departure.
