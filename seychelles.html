An Archipelago Apart

Nature's own composition, thoughtfully explored.

The Seychelles brings together striking granite formations, powder-soft beaches, clear coastal waters and tropical island forests. Each island has its own character, offering a different balance of seclusion, nature and local life.

Kavora helps bring these contrasts together through carefully selected stays, island-hopping possibilities and experiences shaped around your pace and interests.

When to Go

A different perspective with every season.

The Seychelles is a year-round destination, but changing winds and sea conditions can influence swimming, sailing and island transfers. Your preferred activities can help determine the best timing.

Seasonal descriptions are general guidance. Wind, rainfall and sea conditions vary. Confirm current forecasts, marine conditions and inter-island transfer arrangements for your travel dates.
