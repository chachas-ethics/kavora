The spirit of Rwanda

Nature, in its most intimate form.

Rwanda offers a different kind of African journey. Its volcanic mountains shelter endangered mountain gorillas, its eastern savannahs are home to remarkable wildlife, and its ancient rainforests reveal a world of primates, birds, and towering trees.

Three distinct national parks provide the foundation for a journey shaped by nature, meaningful encounters, and time spent in some of the country's most extraordinary landscapes.

Three remarkable places

The Rwanda Collection

From the volcanic peaks of the north to the savannah in the east and the ancient rainforest in the southwest, discover three distinct expressions of Rwanda.

Volcanoes National Park

Set among the dramatic Virunga volcanoes, this is one of the world's most extraordinary places to encounter endangered mountain gorillas in their natural habitat. Mist, bamboo forest, and steep green slopes create an experience that is both physically immersive and deeply memorable.

Known for: Mountain gorilla trekking, volcanic scenery, golden monkeys.

Explore Volcanoes National Park

Akagera National Park

A landscape of open plains, rolling hills, lakes, and wetlands, Akagera reveals Rwanda's classic safari side. Game drives offer opportunities to look for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelope, and the park's reintroduced lion and black rhino populations.

Known for: Big Five safari opportunities, savannah wildlife, lakes and wetlands.

Explore Akagera

Nyungwe National Park

One of Africa's remarkable montane rainforests, Nyungwe is a world of misty canopy, forest trails, and extraordinary biodiversity. Chimpanzee tracking, encounters with other primates, birdwatching, and the famous canopy walkway offer a different rhythm from Rwanda's mountain and savannah safaris.

Known for: Chimpanzee tracking, primates, rainforest walks, canopy walkway.

Explore Nyungwe National Park
