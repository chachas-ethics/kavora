01 / The Destination

A coastline to call your own.

South of Mombasa, Diani Beach stretches along the Kenyan coast with powder-soft sand, turquoise water and a canopy of tropical greenery. Its appeal lies in the balance between natural beauty and the freedom to experience the coast at your own pace.

Spend a morning on the water, discover the reef with a local guide, wander through coastal forest or simply let the hours pass beside the sea. Diani is as much about the space to do very little as it is about the experiences waiting beyond the shore.

03 / Timing Your Visit

The right time to arrive.

Diani’s tropical coastal climate makes it a year-round destination. Weather, wind and sea conditions influence which experiences are most suitable, so timing should reflect the kind of coastal stay you have in mind.

January — March

Often hot and dry

A popular period for beach time and many marine activities. Plan around the heat, sun exposure and local sea conditions.

April — May

Long rains

Rainfall is generally more frequent. Lush scenery and a quieter atmosphere can appeal to some visitors, although outdoor and ocean plans may need flexibility.

June — October

Generally drier

Often a comfortable period for coastal exploration, beach walks and combining Diani with a wider Kenyan itinerary.

November — December

Short rains

Warm weather continues, with the possibility of short rainfall periods. This can be a good time for a coastal escape planned around flexible activities.
