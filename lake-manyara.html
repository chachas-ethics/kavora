Lake Manyara

Discover a landscape of lush forests, open plains, shimmering lakeshore, and remarkable wildlife beneath the dramatic Great Rift Valley escarpment.

Plan Your Lake Manyara Safari

A Landscape of Contrasts

Africa in
Unexpected Forms.

Set beneath the Great Rift Valley escarpment, Lake Manyara National Park brings together a remarkable variety of habitats within a relatively compact landscape. Groundwater forests give way to open plains, while the alkaline lake attracts a wide variety of birds.

Elephants move through the woodland, baboons gather along forest routes, and the park's famously elusive tree-climbing lions have made it one of Tanzania's most distinctive safari destinations. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but every drive offers a chance to observe the landscape changing around you.

With Kavora Africa, Lake Manyara can become a memorable part of a broader northern Tanzania journey or a destination in its own right for travellers drawn to birdlife, forest scenery, and diverse wildlife.

The Manyara Experience

A World of
Quiet Discovery.

Lake Manyara is a destination of contrasts. Its lush forest corridors offer a different atmosphere from the open grasslands and lakeshore, while the escarpment provides a dramatic backdrop to the park's ever-changing scenery.

Game drives provide opportunities to search for elephants, giraffes, buffalo, and other wildlife. Bird enthusiasts can also enjoy the park's rich birdlife, with the species visible depending on the season and water conditions.

Known for: Birdlife, elephants, groundwater forest, lakeshore, and tree-climbing lions.

Planning Your Visit

The Right Time
to Discover Manyara.

Lake Manyara can be rewarding throughout the year. Seasonal rainfall affects the lake, vegetation, birdlife, and overall appearance of the landscape.

Your preferred activities and wider Tanzania itinerary can help determine the most suitable time to visit.

Discuss Your Travel Dates

June – October

Drier Months

Generally drier weather can make this a popular period for safari travel. Wildlife viewing, vegetation, and local conditions vary, so individual sightings cannot be guaranteed.

November – December

Short Rains

Seasonal rainfall may bring fresh vegetation and changing scenery. The timing and intensity of the rains vary from year to year.

March – May

Long Rains

The longer rainy period can bring greener landscapes and atmospheric skies. Some roads and travel arrangements may be affected by rainfall, so flexible planning is useful.

These are general seasonal patterns, not guarantees of weather or wildlife activity. Conditions can vary between years.

A few useful answers to help you plan your Lake Manyara experience.

Ask Us a Question →
What is Lake Manyara National Park known for? +

The park is known for its varied habitats, birdlife, elephants, groundwater forests, lakeshore scenery, and tree-climbing lions. The exact wildlife you encounter depends on conditions and luck.

Can I see tree-climbing lions? +

Lake Manyara is famous for lions that sometimes rest in trees. Sightings are not guaranteed, but a guided game drive gives you the opportunity to search for them.

Is Lake Manyara good for birdwatching? +

Yes. Its lake, woodland, and other habitats support a wide variety of birdlife. The birds you see can vary according to the season and water conditions.

Can I visit Lake Manyara and Ngorongoro together? +

Yes. Lake Manyara and Ngorongoro can be combined in a northern Tanzania itinerary. Your route and time at each destination can be planned around your travel dates and interests.

How do I book a Lake Manyara safari? +

Contact Kavora Africa with your preferred dates, group size, travel interests, and approximate budget. Our team can help you discuss suitable arrangements for your safari.
