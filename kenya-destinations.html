The destination collection

Discover Kenya

Five distinctive settings. Countless ways to experience them. Choose a destination, or let us bring several together into one considered journey.

The Maasai Mara

A landscape of sweeping grasslands, exceptional wildlife viewing, and the possibility of witnessing the great wildebeest migration during its seasonal passage. The Mara is the heart of a classic Kenyan safari.

Best for: Big cats, game drives, migration season, luxury safari camps.

Explore Maasai Mara →

Amboseli National Park

Famous for its elephant herds and striking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, Amboseli offers a different perspective on Kenya's wilderness. Open plains and seasonal wetlands create a remarkable setting for photography and slower safari days.

Best for: Elephants, photography, open landscapes, scenic stays.

Explore Amboseli →

Lake Nakuru

A scenic national park known for rhinos, birdlife, and a lake set among the Rift Valley's dramatic terrain. Lake Nakuru makes a useful wildlife stop on a journey between Nairobi, Naivasha, and the Maasai Mara.

Best for: Rhino sightings, birdwatching, scenic game drives.

Explore Lake Nakuru →

Lake Naivasha

A quieter side of Kenya, where freshwater landscapes, birdlife, boat excursions, and nearby walking experiences offer a welcome change of pace from traditional game drives.

Best for: Boat trips, birdlife, nature walks, relaxed interludes.

Explore Lake Naivasha →

Diani Beach

A beautiful coastal escape with white sand, warm ocean waters, palm-lined shores, and a relaxed atmosphere. Diani offers a contrasting finale to a wildlife journey, with time for the beach, water activities, and restorative stays.

Best for: Beach retreats, honeymoon extensions, ocean activities.

Explore Diani Beach →
