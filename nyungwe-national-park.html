In the mist-covered highlands of southwestern Rwanda, Nyungwe protects one of Africa’s remarkable montane rainforests. Its forested ridges, winding trails and hidden waterways create a landscape that rewards patience and curiosity.

This is a place for a different kind of safari: following chimpanzees through the forest, looking out across the canopy from a suspended walkway, and discovering rare birds and primates with knowledgeable local guides. Every experience begins with respect for the landscape and its wildlife.

Timing your visit

A forest for
every season.

Nyungwe is a rainforest, so rain can occur throughout the year. Drier periods generally make walking conditions easier, while wetter months bring a lush atmosphere and abundant greenery.

For chimpanzee tracking, hiking and canopy walks, allow flexibility for changing weather and trail conditions.

June — September

Drier season

Often a favourable period for hiking and forest walks, with generally more manageable trail conditions. A strong choice for travellers wanting to combine multiple outdoor activities.

December — February

Another drier window

A second period often suited to guided trekking and canopy walks. Pack for cool mornings, changing mountain weather and the possibility of rain.

March — May

Long rains

Expect a particularly green landscape and potentially wetter, muddier trails. Journeys benefit from waterproof layers, sturdy footwear and a flexible itinerary.

October — November

Short rains

Forest conditions can be especially atmospheric, with rain showers and mist adding to the experience. Plan extra time between activities and confirm trail conditions before travel.

Seasonal descriptions are general guidance rather than a weather forecast. Actual conditions vary.

Before you travel

A few things
to know.

A little preparation helps you make the most of Nyungwe’s forest, trails and wildlife experiences.

Nyungwe is known for its ancient montane rainforest, chimpanzee tracking, diverse primate species, exceptional birdlife, hiking trails, waterfalls and suspended canopy walkway.

June to September and December to February are generally drier periods and often favourable for outdoor activities. Nyungwe remains a rainforest, so rain is possible throughout the year.

Yes. Guided chimpanzee tracking is one of Nyungwe’s signature experiences. Sightings are not guaranteed because chimpanzees move through the forest, and the length and difficulty of a trek can vary.

It depends on the activity. Some trails are relatively gentle, while chimpanzee tracking and longer hikes can be physically demanding on steep or muddy terrain. Suitable footwear, rain protection and an honest assessment of fitness are important.

The drive from Kigali generally takes around five to six hours, depending on the route, stops and destination within the park area. A private transfer and an itinerary with suitable rest stops can make the journey more comfortable.

Pack sturdy hiking shoes, lightweight long-sleeved clothing, waterproof outerwear, drinking water and a small daypack. For wildlife activities, follow the park’s current guidance on equipment, conduct and any additional requirements.
