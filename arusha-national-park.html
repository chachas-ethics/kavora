Arusha National Park brings together an unusual variety of landscapes in one compact destination. Forests give way to open grasslands, crater lakes reflect the changing sky, and Mount Meru rises above the surrounding scenery.

Unlike the vast savannahs associated with some of Tanzania's better-known parks, Arusha offers a more varied experience. Its habitats support giraffes, buffalo, numerous bird species, and the striking black-and-white colobus monkey.

Guided walking safaris and canoeing on the Momella Lakes, where available and permitted, offer alternative ways to experience the park. These activities make Arusha a compelling addition to a northern Tanzania itinerary.

The Landscape

Arusha
National Park

From the forested slopes of Mount Meru to the open grasslands and reflective waters of the Momella Lakes, Arusha National Park presents a striking contrast of habitats within a relatively small area.

Giraffes moving across open ground, colobus monkeys among the trees, and changing mountain views give each part of the park its own atmosphere.

Planning Your Visit

Find your
moment in nature.

Arusha National Park can be visited throughout the year. Conditions vary with rainfall, visibility, and the activities you want to include.

If your itinerary includes walking or canoeing, allow time to confirm current availability and conditions when arranging your safari.

Discuss Your Travel Dates

June – October

Drier Months

Often a favourable period for outdoor activities and wildlife viewing, with generally drier conditions. Clear mountain views still depend on local weather.

November – December

Short Rains

Seasonal showers can refresh the vegetation and bring greener scenery. Conditions may change during the day, so plan outdoor activities flexibly.

January – March

Changing Skies

Conditions can offer a mix of green landscapes, mountain views, and occasional rainfall. The experience depends on weather patterns and the timing of your visit.

April – May

Long Rains

Heavier rainfall can create lush scenery but may affect trails, road conditions, and some outdoor activities. Check current conditions before travelling.

These are general seasonal guidelines. Weather, visibility, wildlife activity, and activity availability can vary.

Ask Us a Question →
What is Arusha National Park known for? +
Arusha National Park is known for Mount Meru scenery, forest habitats, crater lakes, giraffes, black-and-white colobus monkeys, birdlife, and opportunities for guided walking safaris and canoeing.
Can I see Mount Meru from the park? +
Mount Meru is a defining feature of the park's landscape. Visibility depends on weather and cloud cover, so clear views are not guaranteed on every visit.
Can I do a walking safari in Arusha National Park? +
Guided walking safaris are available in designated areas subject to park rules, guide arrangements, and current conditions. Walks must follow the applicable safety requirements and park regulations.
Is canoeing available in Arusha National Park? +
Canoeing may be available on the Momella Lakes through authorised operators, subject to current availability, weather, and park arrangements. Confirm this activity when planning your itinerary.
What wildlife can I see in the park? +
Wildlife includes giraffes, buffalo, warthogs, various antelope species, and black-and-white colobus monkeys, alongside a variety of birdlife. Sightings depend on season, location, and conditions.
How long should I spend in Arusha National Park? +
A day visit can provide a rewarding introduction to the park. Travellers interested in combining game drives with walking or canoeing may want to allow additional time, depending on activity availability and their wider itinerary.
Can I combine Arusha with other Tanzania destinations? +
Yes. Arusha National Park can be combined with destinations such as Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Ngorongoro, and Serengeti. The best route depends on your available time, travel plans, and interests.
How do I plan a safari with Kavora Africa? +
Contact Kavora Africa with your intended dates, group size, approximate budget, and preferred activities. You can then discuss an itinerary tailored to your needs and the destinations you would like to visit.
