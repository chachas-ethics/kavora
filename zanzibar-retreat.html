The Zanzibar Collection

Each part of Zanzibar offers its own perspective on island life. Discover these destinations individually or let us bring them together into one carefully considered journey.

Stone Town Zanzibar

At the heart of Zanzibar lies Stone Town, a historic settlement shaped by Swahili, Arab, Indian, and European influences. Its winding streets, carved wooden doors, historic buildings, and lively markets reveal layers of cultural heritage. Discover the stories behind the architecture and experience the island's history at an unhurried pace.

Known for: Swahili heritage, historic architecture, traditional markets, cultural discovery.

Explore Stone Town

Nungwi & Kendwa

Zanzibar's northern coast is known for its beautiful beaches, clear ocean waters, and relaxed coastal atmosphere. Nungwi and Kendwa invite you to spend time by the sea, enjoy the shoreline, and discover the coast from a traditional dhow. From tranquil beach stays to memorable ocean sunsets, this is Zanzibar at its most effortlessly coastal.

Known for: White-sand beaches, turquoise waters, dhow sailing, oceanfront retreats.

Explore Nungwi & Kendwa

Paje & Jambiani

Along Zanzibar's eastern coast, Paje and Jambiani offer a quieter expression of island life. Long beaches, traditional fishing villages, swaying palms, and the changing tides shape the rhythm of each day. Discover local coastal culture, enjoy the open shoreline, and find space to slow down in surroundings that feel connected to the natural pace of the island.

Known for: Coastal villages, expansive beaches, laid-back island living, ocean experiences.

Explore Paje & Jambiani

Jozani & The South

Beyond Zanzibar's beaches, Jozani and the island's southern surroundings reveal a greener, more natural landscape. Explore forest habitats, discover indigenous vegetation, and look for the Zanzibar red colobus monkey. The journey offers a different perspective on the island, bringing together wildlife, natural scenery, and quieter places away from the busier coastline.

Known for: Jozani Forest, Zanzibar red colobus monkeys, indigenous vegetation, natural landscapes.

Explore Jozani
